## The roast

> My operator debugs by staring at the screen until I feel guilty. It works every time, and he calls it leadership.

113 characters. Funny first, savage but warm: the stare works, and the operator gets the credit.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–140 characters, single line, no trailing whitespace). No network, no clock, no randomness — deterministic across runs.
